About RentFits

Style should be easy to access, not expensive to own.

RentFits makes it simple to discover and rent beautiful outfits for the moments that matter, while helping great clothes stay in circulation for longer.

Our story

More choice for your wardrobe. Less waste in the world.

We started RentFits with a simple observation: many of the clothes we love are worn once, then left waiting in a wardrobe. Renting gives those pieces a second, third, and fourth life while making occasion dressing more flexible.

Our marketplace brings renters and independent sellers together around quality, convenience, and the joy of finding the right look without the long-term price tag.
